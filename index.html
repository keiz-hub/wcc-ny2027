Welcome! Let's light up 2027

At midnight on January 1, 2027, every firework launches together. You decide what goes into yours.

  1. Choose a username and a PIN.
  2. Write your New Year Resolution, pick an icon (emoji or picture) and a firework style.
  3. Save it. Edit it anytime before midnight under My entry.
  4. On January 1 at 12:00 AM, tap the icons in the sky to read everyone's resolutions.
